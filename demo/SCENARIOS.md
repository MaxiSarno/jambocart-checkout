# Scenario 1 — Nairobi Mobile Shopper
Country: KE · Currency: KES · Device: Mobile
Amount: $45 · New Customer · Age: 26-40
What should be displayed: M-PESA #1, Airtel Money #2, cards in the background. The core use case of the challenge.

# Scenario 2 — Returning Corporate Buyer (International)
Country: US · Currency: USD · Device: Desktop
Amount: $250 · New Customer · Age: 41+
What should be displayed: Visa #1, Master and PayPal at the top, M-PESA absent. 

# Scenario 3 — Student buying electronics
Country: KE · Currency: KES · Device: Mobile
Amount: $150 · New customer · Age: 18-25
What should be displayed: Lipa Later or Aspira #1 (BNPL rises due to high amount + age), M-PESA in the top 3 but not first. The contrast with scenario 1 is perfect — same country, same device, completely different result.
